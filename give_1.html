Give

“Give, and it will be given to you.” — Luke 6:38

Through faith in Christ we are transformed into generous people. Regular giving helps keep our church alive and reminds us to always put God first.

Ways to give

  • In worship
    Place a gift in the offering plates located in the back of the sanctuary.
  • By mail
    Send gifts to 665 Miller Ave SW, Hutchinson, MN 55350
  • Online
    Set up a one-time or recurring donation at Give online
  • Automatic bank gift (ACH)
    Contact the church office to set up a recurring gift directly from your bank account.
